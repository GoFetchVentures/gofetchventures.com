Website privacy

Effective October 24, 2026

This page covers the gofetchventures.com website, run by Go Fetch Ventures LLC, an Oregon limited liability company. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, which explains what that app collects. For My Poop Fairy, see the privacy policy at mypoopfairy.com.

What this website collects

Nothing you enter. This site has no forms, accounts, cookies, analytics or advertising. Like any website, our hosting provider may briefly log technical details such as your IP address and browser type to keep the site secure and running.

When you email us

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply. We don't add you to a mailing list unless you sign up yourself, and we never sell or share your email.

Links to other sites

Links on this site, such as to mypoopfairy.com, take you to pages with their own privacy policies.

Questions

Email hello@gofetchventures.com. If we change this page, we'll update the date above.
